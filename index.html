About the Club

Rotary E-Club of
All Star CAMANAVA

We are a non-governmental service organization operating primarily online, uniting Rotarians across Caloocan, Malabon, Navotas and Valenzuela. Our e-club format means fellowship and service happen wherever our members are — through digital meetings, shared projects and an active online community.
