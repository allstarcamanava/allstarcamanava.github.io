The Rotary E-Club of All Star CAMANAVA proudly joined the Rotary District 3800 Handover and Induction Ceremonies held on July 4 at the EDSA Shangri-La Manila, celebrating the transition of leadership and the beginning of another meaningful Rotary year.

![](/assets/images/bulletins/handover-1.jpg)


Representing the club were Good President Maria Cristina Chua, who officially turned over the club leadership to High Impact President Dominic Faustino. Joining them were Past President Jemuel Pilapil and PND Rhoda Dasalla.


As one Rotary year concludes and another begins, the ceremony served as a reminder of our shared commitment to Service Above Self and our collective mission to create lasting impact in the communities we serve.


Congratulations to the newly inducted District leaders, and best wishes for a year filled with purpose, collaboration, and impactful service.
