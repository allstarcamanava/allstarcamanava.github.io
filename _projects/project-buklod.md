Project BUKLOD connects children from different backgrounds through interactive learning sessions and community activities, together with the Rotary Club of Plaza Miranda – Quiapo and interfaith partners in Quiapo, Manila.

The project is currently being set in motion, with partners meeting to plan volunteer teachers, learning modules and logistics.
