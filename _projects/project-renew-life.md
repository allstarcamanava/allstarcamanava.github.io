Sometimes, the best stories are the ones that were never part of the original plan. On August 6, 2026, Project RENEW Life supported 148 Persons Deprived of Liberty at the Malabon City Jail Female Dormitory.

The turnover was meant to follow Project BITUIN that day. When inclement weather forced BITUIN to be rescheduled, the club pushed through with Project RENEW Life instead, and what started as a side quest became the main event.

For ReCASC, the heart of Project RENEW Life lies in promoting peace by building bridges with communities that are often unseen, affirming human dignity, and creating spaces where hope and rehabilitation can take root. The club has committed to return and address more of the beneficiaries' needs, especially legal assistance.
