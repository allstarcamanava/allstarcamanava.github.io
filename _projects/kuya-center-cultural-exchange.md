In a celebration of friendship, culture and service, ReCASC joined the Rotary Club of Mandaluyong and its sister club, the Rotary Club of Pudu, Malaysia, in an international service project that promoted cultural appreciation and language learning at Kuya Center in Cubao.

The project showed how Rotary builds bridges across cultures, creating experiences that educate, inspire and unite communities while nurturing global friendship among the next generation.
