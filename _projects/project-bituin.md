Heavy rain and flooded streets did not stop Project BITUIN from bringing health education, essential supplies, and moments of joy to families in Longos, Malabon.

Project BITUIN supports children and families through initiatives focused on maternal and child health, oral hygiene, disease prevention, education, and access to essential resources. With the support of its partners, ReCASC hopes to continue bringing similar activities to more families.
